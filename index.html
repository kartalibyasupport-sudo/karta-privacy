This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how it may be shared, how long we retain it, and the choices and rights available to users.

By using Karta, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Karta, we may collect information necessary to provide and operate the game.

This information may include:

  • Username or display name
  • Profile picture or avatar
  • Internal player ID and account information
  • Game progress, level, achievements, and rewards
  • Match history and game statistics
  • Virtual currency and virtual items
  • Information associated with your Karta account

We may also process technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, language, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, connection information, crash reports, diagnostic information, and information used to detect security problems, fraud, cheating, or abuse.

If you contact our support team, we may process the information contained in your message and any information reasonably necessary to respond to your request.

2. Facebook Login

Karta may allow users to sign in using Facebook Login.

If you choose to use Facebook Login, we may receive information from Meta according to the permissions you grant and the permissions available to Karta. This information may include your Facebook user ID, name, profile picture, and email address where you have granted access to it.

Karta does not receive or store your Facebook password.

Information obtained through Meta platforms is used only for purposes described in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with applicable Meta Platform requirements.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage player accounts
  • Authenticate users and allow account login
  • Save game progress
  • Enable multiplayer and online game functionality
  • Display player profiles and statistics
  • Maintain virtual currency, items, rewards, and achievements
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve game performance and user experience
  • Detect cheating, fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our services
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Where required by law, we will ask for consent before using information for purposes that require consent.

4. Advertising

Karta may display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

Advertising and analytics providers may process limited technical, device, advertising, or usage information in order to provide advertisements, measure their performance, prevent fraud, and improve their services.

Where applicable law requires consent for personalised advertising or tracking, users will be provided with the appropriate choices.

5. In-App Purchases

Karta may offer optional purchases of virtual currency, virtual items, or other digital content.

Payments made through an application store are processed by the relevant payment platform, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store where applicable.

Karta does not directly receive or store users' full payment-card information. We may receive transaction-related information necessary to verify a purchase and provide the purchased digital content.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers where reasonably necessary to operate Karta. These may include:

  • Authentication providers
  • Hosting and server providers
  • Analytics services
  • Crash-reporting services
  • Advertising providers
  • Payment platforms
  • Security and fraud-prevention services

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, court order, legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, our services, or the rights and safety of others.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Account information may be retained while a Karta account remains active. Certain information may be retained for a longer period where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce agreements, or maintain security.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymised.

8. Account and Data Deletion

All Karta users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

To request account or data deletion, email kartalibya.support@gmail.com with the subject Karta Data Deletion Request.

Please provide enough information for us to identify your Karta account, such as your username or player ID. We may request reasonable verification before processing a deletion request in order to prevent unauthorized deletion of another person's account.

Following a valid deletion request, we will delete or anonymise eligible personal information associated with the account as required by applicable law, except for information we are legally required or permitted to retain.

Account deletion may permanently remove game progress, virtual currency, virtual items, achievements, statistics, and other information associated with the account. Once deletion has been completed, some information may not be recoverable.

9. Purchases and Refunds After Account Deletion

Deleting a Karta account does not automatically entitle a user to a refund for previous purchases.

Refund requests are subject to the policies of the platform through which the purchase was made, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store, as well as any applicable consumer protection laws.

Deleting an account may result in the permanent loss of unused virtual currency, virtual items, and other digital content associated with that account, except where applicable law provides otherwise.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

However, no internet service, network, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should never provide passwords, authentication codes, private keys, or other sensitive credentials to anyone claiming to represent Karta.

11. Children's Privacy

Karta does not knowingly collect or process children's personal information in a manner that violates applicable law.

Where parental or guardian consent is required by applicable law, we will take reasonable measures to obtain the required consent.

If we become aware that personal information relating to a child has been collected improperly, we will take reasonable steps to delete or otherwise appropriately handle that information.

Parents or guardians may contact us at kartalibya.support@gmail.com.
