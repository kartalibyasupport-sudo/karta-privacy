User Data Deletion

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Karta - كارطة respects your privacy and gives users the ability to request deletion of their Karta account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your Karta account and personal data:

  1. Send an email to kartalibya.support@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Karta Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include enough information for us to identify your account, such as your Karta username, Player ID, or the email address associated with your account.
  4. If your Karta account is connected to Facebook, you may also include the name or email address associated with your Facebook Login to help us identify the correct account.
We may ask for reasonable verification before deleting an account in order to prevent unauthorized deletion of another user's account.

What Will Be Deleted

After a valid deletion request is verified, we will delete or anonymize eligible personal data associated with your Karta account, which may include:

  • Account and profile information
  • Username, avatar, and player profile data
  • Game progress and level information
  • Match history and game statistics
  • Achievements and rewards
  • Virtual currency and virtual items associated with the account
  • Facebook Login identifiers or other linked login information where applicable
  • Other personal data associated with your Karta account

Information We May Retain

Certain information may be retained where required or permitted by applicable law, or where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, legal compliance, or enforcement of our terms.

Where possible, retained information will be limited to what is necessary and may be anonymized or separated from active account information.

Effect of Account Deletion

Account deletion may be permanent. Game progress, virtual currency, virtual items, achievements, statistics, and other account content may no longer be recoverable after deletion is completed.

Deleting your Karta account does not automatically entitle you to a refund for previous purchases.

Refund requests are subject to the rules of the platform through which the purchase was made, such as Google Play or the Apple App Store, and to applicable consumer protection laws.

Facebook Login Users

If you used Facebook Login with Karta, removing Karta from your Facebook Apps and Websites settings may stop future access through Facebook, but it may not automatically delete data already stored in your Karta account.

To request deletion of data stored by Karta, please follow the deletion request steps above.
